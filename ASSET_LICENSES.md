# Brawl Math portraits
The project owner confirmed permission for this use. Portraits identify named characters in an independent maths exercise. The public policy permits identification and discussion of Supercell products, restricts modification and misleading endorsement, and prohibits new character-based games even when free. Educational use alone does not establish permission. The policy does not specifically address this maths exercise. Supercell may revoke permission.

[English policy](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/) · [Traditional Chinese](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/cnt/) · [Creator Fan Kit](https://creators.supercell.com/en/features/fankit) · [Official Fan Kit](https://fankit.supercell.com/)

The PNGs are unchanged copies from the owner-supplied third-party mirror, with Git blob hashes verified against the pinned revision. Independent verification against the official portal was unavailable. Byron has no matching portrait in this revision and uses the original illustration. Set portraitMode to original in brawl-math/brawlers.mjs to switch all cards back.
